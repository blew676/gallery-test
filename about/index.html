How the records are kept

Every capture lands in folders that AstroTracker watches. It reads each frame's header, measures the subs, and adds up the time per filter, per night and per telescope, leaving out the subs that were rejected.

The record beside each picture comes straight from there. Nothing is typed in by hand, so the hours are the hours.
